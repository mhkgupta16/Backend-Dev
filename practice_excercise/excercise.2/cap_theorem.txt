Exercise 2: CAP Theorem Analysis

Question: For each scenario, identify which CAP properties are
prioritized:

1.  Stock trading platform Answer:

-   CP (Consistency + Partition Tolerance)
-   Accurate data is critical (no wrong trades)
-   System can sacrifice availability if needed

2.  Content delivery network Answer:

-   AP (Availability + Partition Tolerance)
-   Content should always be available
-   Slight inconsistency is acceptable

3.  Airline booking system Answer:

-   CP (Consistency + Partition Tolerance)
-   Prevent double booking of seats
-   Strong consistency is required

4.  Video streaming service Answer:

-   AP (Availability + Partition Tolerance)
-   Video should always play without interruption
-   Minor delays or inconsistency are acceptable
